Play it like an app

Add the Synthesizer to your home screen and it opens full-screen — no browser bars at the top, and the keys fill the whole screen when you turn your phone sideways.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down in the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — then open the Synthesizer from your home screen, just like any app.
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app (on some phones it's Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install — then open the Synthesizer from your home screen, just like any app.

Already added it? Open it from your home screen — that's the full-screen version.

External MIDI keyboard

Not connected. MIDI brings velocity, the sustain pedal, the pitch wheel, and the modulation wheel (which the mod matrix can use). On iPhone/iPad, Safari does not offer Web MIDI — a desktop browser such as Chrome or Edge is needed for a MIDI keyboard.

Record what you play

Nothing recorded yet. Recording captures the finished sound — effects and all — and exports a real WAV file through the share sheet, so it works from the home-screen app too.
